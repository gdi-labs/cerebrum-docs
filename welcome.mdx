## What you get

- **Persistent knowledge.** Ingested from where you work — Slack, Notion, GitHub, Figma, transcripts, email. When engineers leave, context stays.
- **Multi-agent hierarchy.** Planning, execution, and verification are separated across four levels — how reliable engineering teams work.
- **Cost-aware dispatch.** Routine work runs on free local models. Frontier models handle high-value reasoning. Every call is tracked.
- **Audit-ready by default.** Every action emits a typed event. Every call is cost-tracked per tenant. Not a bolt-on — the design center.
