## What you get

- **A team, not a chatbot.** Different agents handle different work — planning, building, reviewing — so every problem reaches the right specialist.
- **Grounded answers.** Every response is anchored to your organization's documents, with citations. No guesses from generic training data.
- **Free and paid models working together.** Routine work runs on free local models. Hard problems escalate to the best paid models. You don't pick — the platform picks per step.
- **Audit-ready by default.** Every step is logged. Every paid call is cost-tracked. Every escalation is recorded.
