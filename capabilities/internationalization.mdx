title: Internationalization (English + Simplified Chinese)
description: '## What it enables'
---

Amos speaks your language. The Command Center is available in English and
Simplified Chinese, and is built to take more:

- Every page is reachable per-language at a short, shareable URL — `/en/atlas`
  and `/cn/atlas`. A link pins its language, so sharing a page shares the
  language it was read in.
- A **language dropdown in the header** — sitting with the alerts and settings
  controls — switches between English and 简体中文 **without leaving the current
  page**, keeping whatever you were looking at, and remembers the choice for
  later visits. Its trigger shows the language you'd switch *to* (`EN` in a
  Chinese UI, `中文` in an English one), so the way out stays legible to someone
  who can't read what's currently on screen.
- First-time visitors get the language their browser asks for: a browser set to
  Chinese opens in Chinese, anything else falls back to English.
- Numbers, dates, currency and weekday names follow the active language, so a
  Chinese page reads `1.2万` and `8月18日` rather than `12K` and `August 18`.

## What's translated today

This first pass covers the app **shell**: navigation, header controls, admin
section tabs, the settings panel, the login page, both not-found pages, and
page titles. Feature-page copy is still English and migrates incrementally.

## What to expect

- **The Chinese copy is a first, machine-translated pass** and still needs a
  human editing pass — wording will improve.
- **Agent-generated prose stays English.** Blurbs, summaries and job output
  produced by the AI workforce render as-is regardless of interface language.
- Feature-page copy (roughly 1,250 strings) and a handful of page titles are
  still English.

Old bookmarks and links keep working — any address without a language prefix
opens in your language. More languages are straightforward to add; the
interface switches itself once a language is registered.
